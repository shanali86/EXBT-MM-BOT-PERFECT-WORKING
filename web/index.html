Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers regarding custody, exchange compliance, and trading execution.

Q1 Do you take custody of our project's treasury or private keys?

Strictly Non-Custodial: Bitfilx Tech operates solely via exchange API keys with Trade-Only permissions. Withdrawal permissions are explicitly forbidden and disabled at the exchange API key level. Your funds, tokens, and USDT remain 100% inside your controlled exchange accounts at all times.

Q2 How does Bitfilx Tech prevent exchange wash-trading or self-trade penalties?

Our multi-account quoting architecture utilizes a proprietary Self-Trade Prevention (STP) Registry. Order IDs, price steps, and execution intervals are strictly synchronized between Account A and Account B so internal orders never match or cross against each other, eliminating wash-trading flags on LBank, Bybit, KuCoin, or Bitget.

Q3 What is the deployment timeline once we approve a package?

Standard engine initialization takes 24 to 48 hours. Once API keys are verified and balance thresholds are confirmed, our quant engineers configure the price corridor, spread targets, and automated watchdog alerts before initiating live quoting.

Q4 How is revenue calculated in the Performance-Based Option?

Under the performance model, the low $2,000 monthly fee covers cloud VPS co-location and risk monitoring. The 10–20% performance share is calculated strictly on verifiable market-making revenue (such as bid/ask spread capture and exchange maker fee rebates), validated via transparent daily ledger audits with zero hidden markups.

Q5 How does the bot defend against macro crypto market dumps?

Our Macro BTC Regime Correlation Engine tracks live Bitcoin volatility on Binance. During high-velocity macro sell-offs, the engine defensively widens bid ladders and activates strict Price Floor circuit breakers to protect project capital from being drained by algorithmic flash crashes.
